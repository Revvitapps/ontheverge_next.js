About

The heart behind the ministry

KD is a teacher, mentor and encourager who believes every person is closer to their breakthrough than they think. For years she has sat with women at kitchen tables, walked young people through hard choices and prayed with families who were ready for a new chapter.

On the Verge Enrichment gives that calling a home: a place to learn, grow and be sent out ready for whatever God is preparing next.
