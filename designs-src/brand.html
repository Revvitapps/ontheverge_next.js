I

The idea behind the mark

The logo already tells a story. Every choice in this guide protects it: a monogram that holds steady, a path of gold that moves toward something, and a star that marks where it's going.

The monogram

The O and V interlock in deep navy and teal. It reads as steady, trustworthy and rooted. This is the ministry's foundation.

The gold path

The swoosh sweeps up and to the right. It's movement, growth and forward motion: the journey from where you are to where you're called.

The guiding star

A four-point star sits just beyond reach. It's the breakthrough you're on the verge of. Use it as the brand's one small signature across the site.

“See, I am doing a new thing! Now it springs up; do you not perceive it?”Isaiah 43:19 · suggested anchor verse

II

Logo

Two lockups and two color treatments. Use the full logo wherever there's room. Use the monogram alone as the favicon, social avatar and small marks.

Clear spaceKeep empty space around the logo equal to the height of the star on every side. Nothing else enters that zone.
Minimum sizeFull logo 160px wide on screen, 1.5 in in print. Below that, switch to the monogram (minimum 32px).
BackgroundsWhite, ivory, mist or Verge Navy. On photos, place the logo over a calm area or a navy overlay at 60% or more.
Files we'll needThe original vector file (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF) if one exists. Today we're working from a JPEG. ✦ Needed from you: vector logo

Please don't

Stretch or squash it
Recolor the navy or gold
Set it on busy patterns
Tilt it or add shadows

IV

Typography

The logo pairs flared Roman capitals with widely spaced caps. Three free Google typefaces carry that voice into every page.

Cormorant GaramondHeadlines
Aa Gg

Elegant, high-contrast serif with a beautiful italic. Use for headlines, quotes and scripture. Weights 400 to 600.

CinzelDisplay caps
AV

Roman inscription capitals, the closest free match to “ON THE VERGE.” Use sparingly: event titles, short headers, numerals.

JostBody & labels
Aa

Clean geometric sans that echoes “ENRICHMENT.” Use for body copy, buttons and spaced uppercase labels. Weights 300 to 600.

V

Spacing & grid

Ministry pages should breathe. Everything sits on an 8-pixel rhythm, and sections get generous room so a visitor never feels rushed.

Grid12 columns on desktop, 4 on phones. Content max width 1240px, reading width about 65 characters.
Gutters16px side margins on phones, 32px on tablets, 48px on desktop.
Sections64px top and bottom on phones, 120px on desktop.
CornersSquare or nearly square (0 to 2px). Rounded pills only for small tags. It keeps the look dignified.
Lines1px hairlines in mist or gold. Gold hairlines may extend from headings like the rules beside “ENRICHMENT.”

VII

Imagery

Real faces from a real congregation. The church is wonderfully diverse, and the photos should look like it: every age, every shade, warm light, genuine moments.

Hero · photo neededKD speaking or praying with a small group, warm natural light
CommunityWomen in a circle with Bibles and coffee
Youth & familyTeens of different backgrounds laughing together
PortraitKD, natural smile, navy or gold wardrobe
GatheringsA full fellowship hall, every age and shade

Look for

  • Candid moments: laughter, prayer, hands holding Bibles and coffee cups
  • Warm, natural light and true skin tones on every complexion
  • Mixed ages and mixed backgrounds in the same frame
  • KD in the center of the work, not posed in front of it

Avoid

  • Generic stock handshakes and staged boardroom smiles
  • Heavy filters that flatten or shift skin tones
  • Photo sets where everyone looks alike
  • Clip-art praying hands, doves and sunbursts

When a photo sits behind text, lay a Verge Navy overlay at 55 to 70% so headlines stay readable. ✦ Needed from you: 8 to 15 photos of real gatherings

VIII

Voice

Write the way KD speaks to someone at the altar or over coffee: warm, sure and hopeful, with Scripture that lands instead of lectures.

Hopeful, not hype

Breakthrough is near, and we say so with calm confidence, not exclamation points.

Personal, not preachy

Speak to “you.” Short sentences. Real-life examples over church jargon.

Rooted, not religious-sounding

Quote Scripture by book and verse, then say plainly what it means for Tuesday.

Words we use

vergenext seasonrootedreadywalk withenrichwholetogether

Words we skip

synergyunlock your potentialblessed and highly favoredlife-changing!!!
Instead ofRegister now for our life-changing transformational workshop!!!
SaySave your seat for Saturday. Bring a friend and a notebook.
Instead ofWe provide holistic enrichment solutions for individuals.
SayWe walk with you, and we don't walk alone.
Instead ofClick here to donate.
SaySow into what's next for a family in our community.
