The heart behind the ministry

Meet KD

KD is a teacher, mentor and encourager who believes every person is closer to their breakthrough than they think. For years she has sat at kitchen tables, led small groups and prayed in hospital hallways with people who needed someone to remind them that God was not finished.

On the Verge Enrichment grew out of that calling: a place where faith meets real life, where women, families and young people are equipped with the Word and with practical wisdom for the season in front of them.
